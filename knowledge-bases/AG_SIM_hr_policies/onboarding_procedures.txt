ACME CORPORATION — NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING PROCEDURES (SIMULATED POLICY DOCUMENT)
Version: 2025.1 | Effective Date: 01 January 2025

========================================================
SECTION 1: ONBOARDING OVERVIEW
========================================================
Welcome to Acme Corporation! Your onboarding programme is designed to help you settle in quickly, understand our culture, and become productive as smoothly as possible.

The onboarding process spans your first 90 days:
  • Week 1    : Administration, system access, orientation.
  • Weeks 2–4 : Role-specific training, team introductions, shadow sessions.
  • Days 30–60: First assigned projects, manager check-ins.
  • Days 60–90: Performance objectives set, full independence.

========================================================

SECTION 3: SYSTEM ACCESS & ACCOUNTS
========================================================
Your IT accounts will be created before Day 1. You will receive an activation email to your personal address.

Day 1 Access Provisioned Automatically:
  • Acme SSO (Single Sign-On) account
  • Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive)
  • Slack (invite sent to work email once SSO is active)
  • HR Portal (hr.acme.internal)
  • Workday (time tracking, expenses, PTO requests)

Access Provisioned by Your Manager (within Week 1):
  • GitHub / GitLab organisation (engineering roles)
  • Salesforce (sales/customer success roles)
  • Jira & Confluence (all teams)
  • AWS / Azure / GCP (engineering; requires security training completion)
  • Any role-specific SaaS tools

IT Helpdesk: helpdesk@acme.com | Ext. 4000 | it.acme.internal

SECTION 6: FIRST-90-DAY MILESTONES
========================================================
Day 30 Check-in (with manager):
  • Review onboarding experience and address gaps.
  • Confirm all paperwork is complete.
  • Set initial OKRs.

Day 60 Check-in (with manager):
  • Progress review on OKRs.
  • Identify any training needs.
  • 360° peer feedback requested.

Day 90 Review (formal):
  • End-of-probation performance review.
  • Confirm permanent employment status.
  • Agree on goals for the next 6 months.

========================================================
SECTION 7: PROBATIONARY PERIOD
========================================================
Duration: 90 days for all new hires.
During probation: standard company policies apply; notice period is 1 week on both sides.
After probation: notice period increases to 4 weeks (or as stated in your contract).
