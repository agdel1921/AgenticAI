ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE (SIMULATED POLICY DOCUMENT)
Version: 2025.1 | Effective Date: 01 January 2025

========================================================
SECTION 1: HEALTH INSURANCE
========================================================
All full-time employees (and their eligible dependants) are covered from Day 1.

Medical Plan Options:
  • Platinum PPO Plan  – Low deductible ($250/individual), wide network. Acme covers 90% of premium.
  • Gold HMO Plan      – Mid deductible ($500/individual), primary-care referrals required. Acme covers 85%.
  • Silver HDHP Plan   – High deductible ($1,500/individual) paired with an HSA. Acme seeds HSA with $600/year.

Dental Plan:
  • 100% coverage for preventive care (cleanings, X-rays).
  • 80% for basic restorative (fillings, extractions).
  • 50% for major restorative (crowns, root canals). Annual maximum: $2,500.

Vision Plan:
  • Eye exam: 100% covered once per year.
  • Frames/lenses: up to $250 allowance per year.
  • Contacts: up to $200 allowance per year.

Enrolment: Must be completed within 30 days of hire via the Benefits Portal (benefits.acme.internal).
Changes outside the enrolment window are only permitted during the annual Open Enrolment period (November each year) or upon a Qualifying Life Event (marriage, birth, etc.).

========================================================
SECTION 2: RETIREMENT & 401(K)
========================================================
Acme Corporation matches employee 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 6% of base salary.

  • Vesting schedule: 25% per year; fully vested after 4 years of service.
  • Employees are auto-enrolled at 3% contribution after 30 days; opt out or change contribution at any time.
  • Investment options include target-date funds, index funds, and a self-directed brokerage window.
  • Provider: Fidelity Investments. Portal: netbenefits.fidelity.com/acme

Roth 401(k) option is also available for after-tax contributions.

========================================================
SECTION 3: LIFE & DISABILITY INSURANCE
========================================================
Basic Life Insurance:   2× annual salary, provided at no cost to the employee.
Supplemental Life:      Up to 5× salary available; employee pays incremental premium.
Short-Term Disability:  60% of salary for up to 12 weeks; kicks in after a 7-day waiting period.
Long-Term Disability:   60% of salary from week 13 until age 65 (subject to plan terms).

========================================================
SECTION 4: EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (EAP)
========================================================
Free, confidential support available 24/7 for all employees and household members:
  • Mental health counselling (up to 8 sessions per issue per year).
  • Legal consultations.
  • Financial planning and debt counselling.
  • Work–life resources (childcare referrals, elder-care support).

Contact: 1-800-555-EAP1 | eap.acme.com

========================================================
SECTION 5: LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
========================================================
  • Annual L&D budget: $2,000 per employee for courses, certifications, and conferences.
  • LinkedIn Learning: unlimited access, fully funded.
  • Tuition Reimbursement: up to $5,250/year for job-relevant degree programmes (requires manager approval and a commitment to remain at Acme for 12 months post-completion).
  • Internal mentoring programme: enrol at talent.acme.internal.

========================================================
SECTION 6: WELLNESS BENEFITS
========================================================
  • Gym/fitness reimbursement: up to $600/year for gym memberships or fitness classes.
  • Mental wellness app (Calm or Headspace): fully subsidised.
  • Ergonomic equipment: one-time $300 allowance for home-office setup.
  • Wellness challenges: quarterly team challenges with prizes.

========================================================
SECTION 7: ADDITIONAL PERKS
========================================================
  • Employee Referral Bonus: $2,000 for a successful hire that stays 6+ months.
  • Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP): Buy Acme stock at a 15% discount; 6-month offering periods.
  • Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax transit/parking up to $315/month.
  • Childcare FSA: Up to $5,000 pre-tax dependent-care FSA.
  • Pet Insurance: Voluntary, discounted group rates through Nationwide Pet.
  • Discount Programme: 10–40% off 500+ national brands via PerkSpot (perks.acme.internal).

========================================================
SECTION 8: HOW TO ENROL / MAKE CHANGES
========================================================
1. Log in to benefits.acme.internal with your Acme SSO credentials.
2. Select "New Hire Enrolment" or "Life Event / Open Enrolment".
3. Review each benefit category and make your elections.
4. Submit elections before the 30-day deadline.
5. Confirmation email will be sent within 2 business days.
