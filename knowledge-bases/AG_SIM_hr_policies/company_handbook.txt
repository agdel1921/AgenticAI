ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SIMULATED POLICY DOCUMENT)
Version: 2025.1 | Effective Date: 01 January 2025

========================================================
SECTION 1: WELCOME & COMPANY OVERVIEW
========================================================
Welcome to Acme Corporation! We are a global technology company headquartered in San Francisco, CA, with offices in New York, London, and Singapore. Our mission is to deliver innovative solutions that transform industries.

Acme Corporation was founded in 1998 and today employs over 10,000 people across 20 countries. We are committed to building a diverse, inclusive, and equitable workplace where every employee can thrive.

========================================================
SECTION 2: CODE OF CONDUCT
========================================================
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary and client information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any potential conflicts to your manager.
- Refrain from discriminatory, harassing, or offensive behaviour.
- Report suspected violations of this code to HR or the Ethics Hotline at ethics@acme.com.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

========================================================
SECTION 3: WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE
========================================================
Standard working hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM (local time).
- Core hours (all teams must be available): 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
- Flexible start/end times are available with manager approval.
- Employees must notify their manager at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start if they will be absent or late.
- Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week; on-site days are Tuesdays and Thursdays by default.

========================================================
SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY
========================================================
Annual Leave: 25 days per year (prorated for part-year starters). Accrues monthly.
Sick Leave: 10 paid sick days per year. No carry-over.
Public Holidays: All national/state public holidays are observed.
Parental Leave: 26 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers; 4 weeks for secondary caregivers.
Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 paid days for immediate family; 2 days for extended family.
Jury Duty: Full pay for the duration of jury service. Notify HR immediately upon receiving summons.

All leave requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at hr.acme.internal.

========================================================
SECTION 5: EXPENSE & REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
========================================================
- Pre-approval required for expenses exceeding $500.
- Submit expense claims within 30 days of incurring the cost.
- Receipts required for all expenses over $25.
- Business travel must be booked through the approved travel portal (travel.acme.internal).
- Per diem rates: Domestic $75/day; International $120/day.
- Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.

========================================================
SECTION 6: IT & DATA SECURITY
========================================================
- All company data must be stored on approved Acme systems; personal cloud storage (Dropbox, personal Google Drive) is prohibited.
- Employees must lock their workstations when stepping away.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days.
- Phishing or suspicious emails must be reported to security@acme.com immediately.
- Company devices must not be used for personal business activities.
- VPN must be used when accessing Acme systems remotely.

For IT support: helpdesk@acme.com | Ext. 4000 | Available 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM Mon–Fri.

========================================================
SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
========================================================
- Annual performance reviews take place in December.
- Mid-year check-ins occur in June.
- Goals are set using the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework.
- Employees rated "Needs Improvement" will be placed on a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan.
- Outstanding performers are eligible for the Acme Excellence Award (cash bonus + recognition).

========================================================
SECTION 8: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
========================================================
Minor infractions: Verbal warning → Written warning → Final written warning → Termination.
Gross misconduct (e.g. fraud, harassment, data breach): May result in immediate suspension and termination without prior warnings.
